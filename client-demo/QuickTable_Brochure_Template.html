Integrated Restaurant Modules

📱 Table QR Digital Menu

Diners scan an acrylic table QR code to view appetizing high-res photo menus with dietary tags, spice levels, and customization notes. No app download needed.

🍳 Kitchen Display System (KDS)

Instant ticket dispatch directly to chef line screens. Real-time preparation countdown timers, audible alerts, and status toggles (Cooking → Ready).

💳 Cashier POS & Billing

Live table bill tracking, 1-click split checks, dynamic UPI payment QR codes, and instant 80mm/58mm thermal receipt printing.

📊 Owner Multi-Branch Command

Monitor hourly sales, best-selling dishes, table occupancy heatmaps, staff access roles, and manage multiple locations from anywhere.
